YANGILASH (muhim — deep-link 404 tuzatildi)
============================================
1) chrome://extensions
2) Radeski GSC Indexer → Remove (o'chirish)
3) Load unpacked → yana shu papka:
   C:\Users\User\Desktop\radeski\scripts\gsc-auto\extension
4) Search Console Overview ni OCHING (radeski.uz) — 404 bo'lmasin
5) Extension → Start

Endi extension inspect?id=... deep-link ochmaydi.
Yuqoridagi "Проверка URL" qatoriga yozib, tugmani bosadi.
